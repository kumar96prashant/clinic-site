Comprehensive Chronic Pain Management

By Dr. Rakhi Goel · Anaesthesia & Pain Specialist (ex-Sir Ganga Ram Hospital) · Goyal Clinics, Yamuna Vihar

Living with chronic pain can be exhausting, affecting every aspect of your life—from your sleep and mood to your ability to work and enjoy time with family. At Goyal Clinics in Delhi, we believe that pain should not dictate your life. Led by Dr. Rakhi Goel, an esteemed pain specialist formerly with Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, our dedicated Pain Management center in Yamuna Vihar provides advanced, minimally invasive treatments for lasting relief.

What is a Pain Management Specialist?

A pain management specialist in Delhi is a medical physician who focuses on diagnosing and treating complex, chronic pain conditions. Dr. Rakhi Goel utilizes a multidisciplinary approach, blending targeted medical interventions with physical rehabilitation to address the root cause of the pain, rather than merely masking the symptoms with oral medications.

Conditions We Treat at Goyal Clinics

Our Yamuna Vihar clinic is fully equipped to handle a wide spectrum of pain conditions, including but not limited to:

  • Sciatica & Slipped Disc: Targeting nerve compression in the lower back that causes radiating leg pain.
  • Trigeminal Neuralgia: Managing severe, shock-like facial nerve pain.
  • Arthritis & Joint Pain: Non-surgical interventions for severe knee, shoulder, and hip osteoarthritis.
  • Post-Surgical Neuralgia: Treating persistent nerve pain following surgeries or trauma.

Advanced Interventional Treatments

Drawing from her extensive experience at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, Dr. Rakhi Goel performs precision-guided interventional procedures. These include:

  • Epidural Steroid Injections: Highly effective for sciatica and herniated discs.
  • Nerve Blocks: Precisely halting pain signals sent by specific irritated nerves.
  • Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA): Using radio waves to create a heat lesion on specific nerves, interrupting pain signals for long-term relief.
