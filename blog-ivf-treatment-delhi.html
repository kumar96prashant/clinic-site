Understanding Infertility Treatments

Infertility can stem from various factors, including PCOS, endometriosis, low sperm count, or unexplained causes. At Goyal Clinics, we offer a stepped-care approach:

  1. Ovulation Induction & IUI: Often the first line of treatment, focusing on timing and minor medical assistance.
  2. IVF (In Vitro Fertilization): The gold standard for many complex fertility issues, where fertilization occurs in our advanced laboratory before embryo transfer.
  3. ICSI: Specifically utilized for severe male factor infertility, ensuring the sperm successfully fertilizes the egg.
